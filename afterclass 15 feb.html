facts about cherry blossom trees

Cherry blossom trees, or sakura, are iconic ornamental trees known for their short-lived (about 1–2 weeks) spring blooms that symbolize renewal and the fleeting beauty of life. Primarily in shades of white and pink, these trees (mostly genus Prunus) are central to Japanese hanami (flower viewing) culture, bloom between March and April, and are a cherished, symbolic gift representing friendship.

The famous cherry trees in Washington, D.C., were given as a gift from Japan in 1912 as a gesture of friendship.
