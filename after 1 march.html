The ICC Men's T20 World Cup is the premier international championship for Twenty20 International cricket, organized by the International Cricket Council (ICC). Featuring 20 teams as of 2024, it is a biennial tournament held every two years where each team plays a single innings limited to 20 overs. It began in 2007.

Qualifying for the T20 World Cup involves a mix of direct entry for top teams from the previous tournament and high ICC rankings, plus regional qualifiers for other nations, with the pathway evolving (e.g., from 16 to 20 teams) to give more opportunities through structured sub-regional and regional events leading to a Global Qualifier or directly to the World Cup. For the upcoming editions, hosts and top finishers from the prior World Cup get automatic spots, while the remaining slots are filled by the next best-ranked teams or through multi-tiered regional tournaments (Africa, Americas, Asia, EAP) to determine the final participants.
